## Reusable listing facts for owner review

FlatWaiver is browser-based digital liability-waiver software for activity businesses. Its $19/month USD plan includes unlimited signed waivers, templates and storage, with a 14-day card-free trial. Businesses review their own wording, publish a signing link, share a QR code or use an internet-connected kiosk, then search records and download stored PDFs. Guardian and group options depend on template configuration. It does not provide legal advice, offline signing, booking synchronization or a guarantee of enforceability. Website: https://www.flatwaiver.com/. Trial: https://www.flatwaiver.com/signup. New pricing and feature-page URLs must not be submitted until approved and deployed.

FlatWaiver and Smartwaiver both collect electronic waivers, but their pricing and operational fit differ. FlatWaiver is $19/month for unlimited waivers. Smartwaiver uses included-volume plans; its published October 8, 2026 update is now effective. Choose around your arrival workflow, record retrieval and integrations as well as cost.

## A practical comparison

| Requirement | FlatWaiver | What to verify with Smartwaiver |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public phone signing | Share a link or QR code; no signer account needed | Test your public form and required fields |
| Reception device | Browser-based kiosk; internet required | Native app and offline requirements |
| Existing PDF | Convert to an editable draft, review, then publish | Conversion and setup assistance |
| Minors | Configured participant and guardian fields | Your guardian workflow and legal requirements |
| Records | Search, stored signed PDF, CSV export | Bulk export and cancellation access |
| Booking and CRM | No automatic booking synchronization promised | Your exact integration and eligible tier |

## What the subscription comparison means

At 1,000 waivers, Smartwaiver Business is $160/month under the effective update. FlatWaiver is $19. That is $1,692 less over twelve months at unchanged monthly rates, before taxes, add-ons and feature differences. At 100 waivers, Smartwaiver Basic is $22. Use [the calculator](/#compare) for the verified allowance tiers; it does not estimate custom plans or upgrade invoices.

## When staying with Smartwaiver can make sense

If your business relies on native offline collection, an established booking integration or a particular check-in process, document that dependency before changing tools. Verify it directly with Smartwaiver and rehearse the equivalent process during any trial. A cheaper subscription does not recover the cost of a broken arrival workflow.

## When to evaluate FlatWaiver

FlatWaiver fits teams that want a reviewed waiver online, a link or QR code, a connected tablet option and searchable records at a flat monthly price. Publishing creates a new immutable version. Signed records retain the original version and stored PDF. Neither conversion nor evidence capture is legal advice or a guarantee of enforceability.

Test a [gym arrival](/industries/fitness-studios), [climbing visit](/industries/climbing-gyms) or [family arrival](/industries/trampoline-parks) with synthetic participants. Do not send live visitors to a new link until staff have practiced retrieval and guardian handling.

## Switch without losing historical agreements

Export existing documents before cancelling any provider. Preserve their original evidence and identify how staff will retrieve them. Converting a template does not migrate or recreate old signatures. Replace your own website links, printed QR signs and message templates deliberately; historical provider URLs will not automatically become FlatWaiver URLs.

Waiver software pricing depends on the billing model, included volume, overages and features. FlatWaiver is $19/month for unlimited signed waivers, templates and storage. Other products use included-volume tiers or a base fee plus usage. Compare the total for your own quiet and busy months before choosing.

## Build a comparable monthly budget

1. Count expected signed waivers, not visits: a returning guest may already have a record, while a family can have several participants on one document.
2. Ask each vendor how it counts a group form, voided submissions and repeat signatures.
3. Compare a monthly subscription with another monthly subscription. Annual commitments and transaction-priced booking services belong in separate calculations.
4. Add usage, overages, storage, devices, seats and integrations where your workflow needs them.
5. Test export and cancellation access before entrusting your records to a platform.

## Three pricing models

**Included-volume subscriptions:** Smartwaiver and WaiverFile have published monthly allowances. The calculator on our [comparison section](/#compare) selects the lowest published subscription that covers the selected volume. It excludes overage strategies, negotiated rates and add-ons.

**Base plus usage:** WaiverForever lists Essential, Standard and Scale base plans plus usage after included submissions. Its total depends on both the feature plan and submission charges. WaiverSign publishes $0.15 per additional waiver beyond the selected allowance. An allowance tier is not necessarily the cheapest combination once overages are considered.

**Flat subscription:** [FlatWaiver's monthly plan](/pricing) keeps its waiver subscription at $19 when volume grows. It does not provide booking, membership payments, offline signing or legal drafting. A lower waiver subscription is not evidence that it replaces a complete venue-management system.

## Worked example: 1,500 waivers per month

Smartwaiver Premium's verified 2,500 allowance costs $205/month under the effective October 8 schedule. FlatWaiver is $19/month. At unchanged rates for twelve months, the difference is $2,232. This is an annualized comparison, not a prediction of a mixed-rate calendar-year invoice.

WaiverFile lists $249/month for a 4,000 allowance, or $149 for 1,300 plus $0.50 for each extra waiver. At 1,500, both calculations are $249 before add-ons. WaiverSign's $189 plan includes 2,500; another plan plus overages may fit a different volume better. WaiverForever needs a separate base-plus-usage estimate from its calculator.

## Where a flat plan may not fit

A suitable free plan can cost less for very light usage. A venue that needs native offline collection, booking integration or payment processing should budget for those capabilities, not compare signature counts alone. Ask what remains available after cancellation and download a sample export during your trial.

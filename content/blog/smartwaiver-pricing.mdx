Smartwaiver's published monthly prices now start at **$22 for Basic**, under the update effective October 8, 2026. The six verified allowance tiers below cover up to 2,500 waivers per month. Premier is $280/month, with its allowance requiring confirmation; Enterprise is quoted separately.

## Which plan fits your volume?

Use your busiest expected month as well as an ordinary month. At 120 waivers, the verified Growth allowance covers the volume at $41/month. At 400, Professional covers it at $110. At 1,000, Business is $160. At 1,200, Premium is $205. These are published full-month subscription rates, not an estimate of your immediate upgrade invoice.

Upgrading starts a new billing cycle, with credit for unused days on the previous plan. Confirm the amount due and the renewal date in your account before changing plans. The [official limit guidance](https://support.smartwaiver.com/hc/en-us/articles/360056591972-What-happens-if-I-go-over-my-waiver-limit) explains the upgrade process.

## What changed in October 2026?

Smartwaiver announced the change on September 8, 2026. Existing subscriptions move to the new rate on the applicable billing or renewal date on or after October 8. The notice says packaging and features did not change and Enterprise is excluded. Older $19 Basic and $155 Business references describe the previous schedule, not the current comparison.

The public pricing page can lag the dated support notice. This guide uses the [official price-update notice](https://support.smartwaiver.com/hc/en-us/articles/48619421464845-Smartwaiver-Subscription-Pricing-Update-Overview-FAQs) for effective rates. Confirm your account's actual offer and included volume before purchase.

## Monthly versus annual billing

Annual subscriptions are a different commitment. The notice specifies $210/year for Basic, $395 for Growth and $575 for Starter. Do not treat annual billing as a cancellable monthly subscription, or infer unlisted annual tiers from a percentage discount. Ask about tax, storage, integrations and seasonal access separately.

**Smartwaiver's pricing runs from $19/month (up to 100 signed waivers) to $199/month (up to 2,500), with a $7/month storage plan for the off-season.** It's usage-based: your bill is set by how many waivers you collect, and every plan includes all the features. This is the full Smartwaiver pricing breakdown for 2026 — every tier, the *real* cost per waiver, and the fees that don't show up on the headline price.

## Smartwaiver pricing at a glance

| Plan | Monthly price | Signed waivers / month | Cost per waiver (if you fill the tier) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | $19 | up to 100 | ~$0.19 |
| Starter | $55 | up to 300 | ~$0.18 |
| Business | $155 | up to 1,000 | ~$0.16 |
| Premium | $199 | up to 2,500 | ~$0.08 |
| Storage | $7 | — (paused) | retain records while not collecting |

Above 2,500 waivers a month, Smartwaiver moves to custom/enterprise pricing. Every tier includes the full feature set — you're paying purely for volume.

## The real cost per waiver

The "cost per waiver" column above is the *best case* — it assumes you fill the tier exactly. Almost nobody does, and that's where the effective price climbs.

You pay for the tier's **ceiling**, not your actual count. Collect 400 waivers in a month and you're on the Business plan (Starter caps at 300), so those 400 waivers cost **$155 — about $0.39 each**, more than double the headline rate. Collect 120 and you're on Starter at $55, or **~$0.46 per waiver**. The closer you sit to the *bottom* of a tier, the worse your per-waiver cost gets.

This is the core of volume pricing: your bill is a function of your busiest month, not your average one.

## What one extra waiver can cost you

"Upgrade anytime" sounds friendly, but crossing a tier boundary re-prices your entire month. The marginal cost of a single waiver at each boundary:

| Waiver # | Moves you from | To | Extra per month |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 101st | Basic ($19) | Starter ($55) | **+$36** |
| 301st | Starter ($55) | Business ($155) | **+$100** |
| 1,001st | Business ($155) | Premium ($199) | **+$44** |

Signing your 301st customer in a month adds $100 to that month's bill for one signature. For a growing gym or park, a single strong weekend can bump you a tier — and the increase sticks for the whole billing period.

## The $7/month storage plan

Smartwaiver's storage plan is aimed at seasonal businesses: when you're not actively collecting waivers, you can drop to **$7/month** to keep access to your existing records rather than paying a full tier.

It's a sensible option — but read what it means. Your signed waivers are legal records you may need years later, and retaining access to them carries an ongoing fee even in your off-season. Budget for it as a year-round cost, not a pause.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does Smartwaiver cost?** From $19/month (100 waivers) up to $199/month (2,500), plus a $7/month storage plan for periods when you're not collecting. Above 2,500/month it's custom pricing.

**Does Smartwaiver charge per waiver?** Not as a per-waiver overage fee — it's tiered. When you exceed your plan's monthly allowance, you move up to the next tier for that period, which re-prices the whole month.

**What is the Smartwaiver storage plan?** A reduced $7/month plan that keeps your existing signed waivers accessible when your account isn't actively collecting — useful for seasonal operators.

**Is there a free Smartwaiver plan or trial?** There's a free trial (no card required, per Smartwaiver's site). There isn't an indefinitely free paid tier — the entry plan is $19/month.

**What happens if I go over my plan's waiver limit?** You move to the next tier up for that billing period, so a single busy month can raise your cost until you drop back down.

## The bottom line

Smartwaiver's pricing is transparent about its tiers but quiet about the two things that actually shape your bill: you pay for the tier ceiling rather than your real volume, and crossing a boundary re-prices the whole month. For low volume it's inexpensive; for a high-traffic venue, the tiers add up fast.

Smartwaiver pricing in 2026 ranges from **$19/month for Basic to $270/month for Premier** under the current monthly schedule through October 7. Announced prices effective **October 8, 2026** range from **$22/month for Basic to $280/month for Premier**; the appropriate plan depends on how many signed waivers you collect. Enterprise pricing is separate and unaffected, and existing subscribers move to the new rates on their applicable billing or renewal date on or after October 8.

## The real cost per waiver

Effective cost per waiver is the monthly plan price divided by actual signatures collected. These examples assume a full month on the selected plan, before taxes or credits.

At 400 waivers, Professional covers the volume: **$105 currently, approximately $0.26 per waiver**, or **$110 under October rates, approximately $0.28**. At 120 waivers, Growth covers the volume: **$37 currently, approximately $0.31 per waiver**, or **$41 under October rates, approximately $0.34**.

Choose the verified plan that covers your expected volume; do not skip intermediate tiers when comparing costs.
